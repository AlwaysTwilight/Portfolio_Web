Procedural (built in Three.js code, no external model — no suitable free/
downloadable CC0 asset was found for a gaming chair or a curved monitor):
  - Gaming chair (bucket seat, side bolsters, headrest, 5-star wheel base)
  - Curved monitor (bent screen segment + stand)
  - Record player, pencil, wall clock

The mini desk robot reuses /models/robot_mascot.glb, the same asset already
used by the hero section's robot mascot.

CC0 assets are free for personal, educational, and commercial use with no
attribution required. Credit to Kenney (www.kenney.nl) is appreciated.
